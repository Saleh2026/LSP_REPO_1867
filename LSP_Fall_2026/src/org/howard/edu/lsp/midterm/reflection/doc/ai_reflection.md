Section 1: Benefits (3 points)
-
AI tools have been a huge part of my learning process this semester. Both in and outside of this class I have found, in certain cases, that it can save me a lot of time by proposing more real world examples that connect closer with my life experiences. One thing that I've always struggled with has been connecting to educational content when I feel it's impact isn't reflected in my life. Without those real world examples, it becomes all too easy to question the necessity of learning certain things and my brain will automatically start to question whether what I'm doing is a good use of time.

Within this course specifically AI was a enormous help with grasping the need and implementations of Heuristics. When the concepts were initially presented through abstract algorithms and formal definitions, it was hard to appreciate why we would ever choose an 'educated guess' or an approximation over an exact mathematical solution. By prompting AI tools to map heuristics to situations I already navigate—like budgeting time during finals, triaging a crowded inbox, or choosing a driving route during rush hour—the concept clicked instantly. It stopped feeling like an academic shortcut and started looking like an indispensable engineering strategy for managing computational complexity and finite resources in the real world.

Section 2: Challenges (3 points) 
-
One situation that is all too common is when AI misinterprets certain key words. This actually happened to me recently when I was trying to use AI to come up with pertinent questions for a Photo shoot request/planning form. I asked for a 'photo shoot planning form, step-by-step'. Instead of giving me tips on questions or suggestions on a forms platform, Gemini returned 5 pages on 'How to build an HTML Framework'. 

This taught me that inferred meaning between words is not understood by AI and after more clearly laying out what I was looking for, I was able to get the outcome I wanted, a fully formatted and polished photo shoot request/planning form.
