In this Library Lending System, `Library` collaborates with `Book` because `Library` is responsible for maintaining the collection of books, finding books by ISBN, and coordinating borrowing and returning operations by delegating individual availability state transitions to each `Book` instance. Conversely, `Book` has no collaborators and does not reference `Library` because its responsibilities are strictly limited to storing its own metadata (`isbn`, `title`, `author`), maintaining its own availability state, and validating single-book state changes (`borrowBook()` and `returnBook()`). Keeping `Book` independent of `Library` preserves low coupling and allows standalone `Book` objects to be instantiated, borrowed, and returned outside of a `Library` context. Finally, `Library` enforces the three-book borrowing limit because that rule governs the aggregate state across the entire collection of books rather than any single `Book` instance, aligning directly with `Library`'s CRC responsibility to enforce collection-wide lending policies.
